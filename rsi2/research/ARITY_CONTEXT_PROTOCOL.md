# Generic arity contexts: prospective TRAIN-only diagnostic

Registered before executing the new diagnostic. The completed coordinate
comparison, its source programs, journals and independent replay remain frozen
historical evidence. This revision opens TRAIN only. No VALID, TEST or external
audit partition is used, and no learning or final RSI success is assessed here.

## Eleven-field prospective cycle record

1. **Current performance.** The completed B64 coordinate diagnostic and matched
   population controls solve the same 5/36 TRAIN tasks under both cold and
   prior-bank conditions. At B640, cold coordinates solves 5/36 and cold
   population solves 6/36; coordinates reaches the compiler cap on 31 unsolved
   tasks and population on 30. Those results were independently replayed and
   do not qualify the coordinate kernel as an improvement.

2. **Failure clusters.** Coverage inspection of B64 coordinate candidates found
   96 occurrences of integer binary application spines, comprising 16 distinct
   ASTs. All were variable-bearing and occurred exclusively in six list-to-int
   searches, through eta-exposed fold reducers. List-to-list and list-to-bool
   searches contained zero such spines. This is a narrow observation about the
   measured candidate stream; it neither proves general expressivity failure
   nor identifies a missing target program.

3. **Structural bottleneck.** Existing head changes, argument permutations and
   unary insertion/removal can preserve or rearrange available argument trees,
   but do not directly insert a general multi-argument context around an own
   subtree while synthesizing its other arguments. The coverage measurement
   motivates testing that missing local construction. Active compiler limits
   also constrain how much construction can be attempted.

4. **Why prior attempts were insufficient.** The frozen coordinate comparison
   shows no B64 solved-set expansion with either cold roots or verified bank
   reuse, and loses one task to population at B640. Faster execution, additional
   variable-bearing ASTs and a larger candidate allowance did not establish a
   verified capability increase. Probability ordering and structural operator
   coverage are separate hypotheses and require separate switches.

5. **Intervention.** Add generic application contexts from every compatible
   current-grammar production of arity at least two. Retain the source subtree
   in every jointly compatible argument role and enumerate the remaining holes
   from the same grammar under shared type constraints and the original binder
   environment. Use budgeted generic enumeration, including existing generic
   eta exposure where enabled. There are no primitive-specific or task-specific
   construction cases, supplied target ASTs, added primitives, new literal
   values or hand-set learned weights. Round-robin agendas cover all eligible
   heads and retained-argument roles. Each context first enumerates a finite
   atomic-filler layer, then arbitrary typed best-first filler derivations.
   An atom is every compatible arity-zero grammar production, including bound
   variables, primitive/library function leaves and frozen constants; no
   bespoke subset or order is supplied. Both layers propagate shared type
   constraints and debit the same structural/compiler meters and CPU guard.

   This scheduling revision was chosen on synthetic examples before any new
   corpus run. The initial unrestricted filler enumeration exhausted 20,000
   compiler units before producing a context candidate for scalar identity.
   The generic atomic layer produced eight candidates in 840 compiler units,
   including `(lambda x. add x x)`. These figures test construction mechanics
   only and confer no TRAIN capability credit.

   The independent flags are `arity_context` and `production_priority`. Both
   false preserve the legacy coordinate candidate stream and its work path.
   Priority true orders actual grammar production probabilities using recovered
   parent/argument contexts. Uniform probabilities preserve the candidate order
   wherever both executions reach the same prefix; context recovery and sorting
   cost additional metered work and can shorten a compiler-capped execution.
   The present diagnostic keeps priority false in every condition.

6. **Expected mechanism.** Retaining an own typed subtree while constructing
   compatible sibling arguments may reach useful complete programs absent from
   the old local neighborhood within the same candidate allowance. This is a
   falsifiable engineering hypothesis. More contexts, different ASTs, better
   public error or faster CPU alone do not satisfy the admission criterion.

7. **Verification plan.** Run `arity_diagnostic_run.py` on frozen TRAIN36, seed
   11, with four conditions in this fixed order: `cold_baseline`, `cold_arity`,
   `bank_baseline`, `bank_arity`. The baseline is the unchanged coordinate
   kernel. Each condition receives a fresh default Grammar, frozen zero scorer,
   the same root allocation policy and initial fresh-prefix allowance of 16,
   the same task order and B64 complete-candidate ceiling. Use size 12, 2,000
   interpreter steps per evaluation, 20,000 structural units, 20,000 compiler
   units and ten CPU seconds per search. All phases share one aggregate ceiling
   of 600 CPU seconds, including source loading, verification, workers,
   persistence and parent reconstruction. Record physical work and elapsed
   times; equal candidate ceilings do not imply equal computation.

   Cold and prior-bank results are compared separately. The bank contains only
   original verifier-attributed raw programs from the frozen seed-11 recursive
   pilot, independently reverified before use with original provenance.
   No fitted library, recognition state or production weights enter this run.
   Use a new empty output directory; preserve completed records on interruption

8. **Regression risks.** Filler enumeration may spend the compiler allowance
   before productive contexts are emitted, starve older edits, duplicate roots
   or increase runtime-invalid candidates. Joint polymorphic constraints,
   library references and de Bruijn binder origins must remain correct.
   Provenance must reconstruct every retained source and generated filler.
   Priority overhead may alter capped prefixes even under uniform weights.
   Synthetic checks address mechanics; TRAIN verification addresses only the
   finite registered examples, not equivalence over all inputs.

9. **Result after testing.** Prospective diagnostic unrun; new solved sets,
   regressions and resource outcomes are UNKNOWN. The historical coverage
   measurement and old verified 5/36 result are motivation, not evidence that
   the proposed arity contexts work. Learning, later-generation reuse, original
   RSI criteria (a)–(e) and final generalization remain unassessed.

10. **Keep/revert/revise decision.** Retain a passing arity kernel only as an
    engineering candidate for a separately registered learning campaign.
    Equality, any lost baseline task in the claimed comparison, proxy-only
    gains, incomplete verification or an incomplete aggregate run reject that
    comparison's admission claim. Preserve failures and exact journals as
    diagnostic memory; do not relabel them as accepted programs or learned
    improvement. Subsequent revisions require a new prospective record.

11. **Updated reusable rule.** A measured candidate-coverage gap can motivate a
    generic operator change, but capability credit requires a complete matched
    no-loss solved-set expansion independently verified at the registered
    limits. Structural extensions and learned probability updates remain
    independent factors. Any eventual failure-credit campaign must apply the
    same retained kernel to every matched control and learned arm, verify an
    engine-learned change against its same-state predecessor and controls, and
    reuse that admitted change in a later generation before claiming loop
    improvement. This diagnostic does not open final evaluation partitions.
