# Recursive bootstrap development pilot

This TRAIN-only pilot tests whether verified experience becomes a better
improvement procedure. It does not replace the original failed a–e study.
The original TEST, validation and reporting audit are inaccessible to the
pilot; fresh final evaluation remains sealed until a development system and
its acceptance tests are frozen.

All arms share a human-designed typed population kernel. Initial parents come
from its own frozen enumeration and prior-generation verifier-accepted TRAIN
programs. Their own generated descendants may receive further typed edits,
with parent and replacement provenance. Only selected complete solutions
passing unchanged TRAIN public and hidden verification become learning or
library labels. The prior-generation bank snapshot prevents task-order leakage.

Alternate guided and FIFO parent turns. An incumbent synthesized DSL scorer
receives complete public outputs (empty on failure), targets, size and depth,
under the unchanged four-argument contract. It chooses which parent generates
future edits; it cannot retroactively rerank an already charged solution.
Zero/constant scoring preserves the common scheduler. Charge all candidates,
interpreter calls/steps, heuristic executions/steps, edit enumeration pops,
normalization work and physical CPU. No shared evaluation cache crosses arms.

Keep seeds11/22/33, B64, BRUTE640 B640, size12, expansion20,000 and step2,000.
Use at least eight generations in a completed campaign. Controls are BASE,
BRUTE640, ONESHOT, FULL, NO_LIBRARY, NO_RECOGNITION and NO_HEURISTIC. BASE and
BRUTE freeze initial learning state; ONESHOT freezes after generation1; FULL
and ablations update at each generation. Raw accepted ASTs provide explicit
bank provenance; verified executable rewrites provide grammar/recognizer
labels. Generic extraction must strictly reduce total real-solution plus
library AST size and reverify every retained task. Compression or proxy loss
alone never establishes useful library contribution.

The initial proposal provider preserves four own-grammar enumerations and
two seeded mutations. It may be replaced only by a separately registered,
generic own-grammar synthesis method after a diagnosed development failure.
Screen first four name-sorted TRAIN tasks at B16; confirm two candidates on
all TRAIN at B64. Require a strict hidden-verified solved-set expansion with
no loss before adopting a valid, informative scorer. Every admission record
precedes its use in the next wake. The fixed screen is development feedback,
not a generalization test. A partial pilot cannot satisfy eight generations.

First run seed11 FULL plus paired same-state zero-scoring comparisons where
feasible, under a score-blind 600 aggregate CPU-second watchdog. A failed or
partial pilot triggers a new eleven-field diagnosis and separately registered
revision; it must not trigger final TEST queries. A later complete campaign
requires its own aggregate ceiling and full common-kernel controls before
one locked final evaluation of criteria a–e and causal learned-library,
recognizer, heuristic and improvement-procedure effects.
