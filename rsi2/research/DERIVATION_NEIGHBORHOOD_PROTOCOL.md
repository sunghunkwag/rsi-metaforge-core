# Typed derivation neighborhoods: TRAIN-only revision

Registered before running the new method. The existing recursive pilot and
its frozen source remain separate evidence. No VALID, TEST or external audit
tasks may be loaded by this revision.

## Diagnosis and hypothesis

The recursive pilot's completed generation0 and generation1 wake each solve
5/36 TRAIN tasks. Its accepted identity abstraction reduces real AST MDL
14 to13 but increases verification interpreter work1115 to1193 steps. Neither
is a verified capability gain. Earlier replacement, observational and sampling
experiments also failed to expand the frozen baseline's solved set reliably.
Arbitrary whole-subtree replacement loses existing sibling evidence, while
constant-first closed wrappers delay useful bound-variable orientations.

The intervention replaces that edit generator with a typed derivation
neighborhood. It operates on its own enumerated or verifier-accepted ASTs;
there are no supplied task programs, task-name switches, new primitives,
literal values, hand-written DSL scorers or hand-set production weights.

## Candidate production

Generate maximal application-spine head substitutions from the current
grammar, preserving the existing arguments. Enumerate compatible argument
permutations with jointly checked substitutions. Generate compatible unary
insertions/removals from the same grammar. Generic eta exposure of partial
function spines may expose their bound input to these operators; equivalent
intermediates are charged structural/compiler work and are not separately
evaluated. The complete final candidate remains within the frozen size12
limit. Record the parent AST, source path, production, argument mapping,
intermediate transforms and exact final AST so every edge is reconstructable.

Round-robin agendas provide fair coverage of operators, locations and parents.
The incumbent DSL scorer keeps its existing causal parent-selection role.
All candidates remain jointly well typed in their original binder scope.
Every unique complete attempted candidate consumes the shared B; all public
interpreter calls/steps, heuristic executions, generated structural states,
normalization visits, duplicates and physical CPU are counted. Structural
generation has a shared cap20000 and compiler work cap20000. Selected roots
must pass unchanged, independent public AND hidden TRAIN verification.

## Initial diagnostic and admission

Use identical frozen grammar, original TRAIN36, seed11, B64 and B640, size12,
step2000 and ten CPU seconds per search. Compare the existing population
method and the new neighborhood under the same initial root-prefix and
verified prior-bank conditions; cold and bank-conditioned comparisons are
reported separately. Never call a partial prefix a complete campaign.
The aggregate diagnostic ceiling is600 CPU seconds; timings include physical
search and verification work, and persistence overhead is reported separately.
Unrun seeds22/33 and any controls remain UNKNOWN.

Reject a method that loses previously verified tasks or merely improves MDL,
proxy error or CPU timing. If it produces a strict verified TRAIN solved-set
expansion, retain it only as a candidate kernel for a separately registered
recursive campaign and test all seeds and common-kernel controls there.
Human-designed operators are object-level engineering. Only a subsequent
engine-learned change to generating, evaluating or selecting interventions,
causally verified against its predecessor and reused next generation, can
support a loop-level claim. Original RSI criteria a-e and generalization remain
unassessed until a frozen complete system is independently evaluated.

## Required cycle record

Record performance, failure clusters, structural bottleneck, why previous
attempts were insufficient, intervention, expected mechanism, verification
plan, regression risks, observed result, keep/revert/revise decision and the
updated reusable rule. Failed candidates remain diagnostic memory, never
verified library labels. Successful rules must name their measured scope.

## Matched diagnostic execution order

Under the single600 aggregate CPU-second diagnostic ceiling, run four B64
conditions in this order: cold population, cold coordinates, prior-bank
population, prior-bank coordinates. Both receive the same frozen initial
Grammar, root-reservation policy, zero scorer, seed and task order. The bank
contains only raw verified programs from the earlier frozen recursive pilot;
all are independently reverified before use and carry their original proof IDs.
No fitted recognition/library/weights from that pilot enter this comparison.

If a paired B64 condition shows a strict verified solved-set expansion without
loss, choose bank if its condition passes, otherwise cold, for the two matched
B640 follow-ups. Otherwise use cold B640 to diagnose search-space coverage.
This development-stage choice never opens heldout evaluation. The watchdog
shares its remaining budget across all phases; unstarted or truncated phases
stay unrun/partial. New journal persistence preserves all completed trials and
proofs; its physical work belongs to the same ceiling. Structural units for
coordinate choice examination differ from heap-pop units, so the comparison
claims equal candidate budgets and reports both work/CPU measures rather than
asserting equal physical search work.
