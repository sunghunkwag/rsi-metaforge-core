# The design panel of 2026-11-16: what follows the open design

After the registered discovery of the open design had run 29 steps without an acceptance and design
q had gained nothing in development, the next technique was not chosen by the session alone. Five
designer agents, each given one angle, proposed a technique from the same brief (`BRIEF.md`: the
measured facts, the constraints, the code); three judge agents scored all five, each weighting one
lens (power and statistics; rigor and validity; feasibility and novelty), checking every
quantitative claim against the brief and the code; one synthesis agent chose and specified. The
agents read an isolated copy of the package and wrote only in their own directories; none ran a
learner. Their structured returns are kept here as returned (scratch paths written `<scratch>/`).

| file | what |
|---|---|
| `design_statistics.json` | race-and-panel acceptance: noise-allocated sequential screening and a known-variance confirmation |
| `design_generator.json` | a pooled posterior of edit effects building macro-edits sized to the acceptance threshold |
| `design_level.json` | recursive discovery inside acquisition with an amortised discoverer across categories |
| `design_unconventional.json` | self-model discovery: learning principles searched inside a recorded model of the learner's own acquisition landscape |
| `design_decisive.json` | the relay of the learner's inner-discovery record across categories, decided once on unseen categories |
| `judge_0.json`, `judge_1.json`, `judge_2.json` | scores (power, rigor, novelty, feasibility, honesty), fatal flaws, errors found in the proposals |
| `synthesize.json` | the chosen design, the grafts, the specification, the power analysis, the predictions, the development checks |

All three judges ranked the relay design (decisive) first; the synthesis chose it, with changes the
judges asked for (a uniform-permutation placebo instead of a derangement, a credit rule that ignores
ties, more chains, m_net defined, a retention endpoint) and grafts from the other proposals (a block
library, an edit-size prior, a futility look, a priority list under a CPU cap). The panel was
convened before the Owner's instruction 14 arrived; its brief and the synthesis's copy of the
directive were updated with it while the judges worked. Every judge noted the same limit: the
mechanism is modest (credit-assigned operator selection carried across categories), and the most
probable outcome the synthesis predicts is a decisive negative. That is what this attempt is
registered to find out, on categories no process has used.
